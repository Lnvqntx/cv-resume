\entry{AlphaNova Biweekly Competition, Season 1}{Cycle 4: rank 21 (star signal); Cycle 5 ongoing}
      {Python, XGBoost}{}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Forecast a hidden cross-sectional target on 20 assets, scored by Sharpe after a 5 bp turnover cost. Built 15
        candidate predictors, a walk-forward harness, an overfitting-gate simulator and automated leakage, determinism
        and timing checks.
  \item Submitted a factor-hedged reversal signal (PCA-neutralised, with an XGBoost overlay) that passed the novelty and
        overfitting gates; reproduced the platform's novelty metric offline (25.5$^\circ$ vs 25.49$^\circ$ on the site). A star marks a
        prize-eligible signal (correlation of at most 0.5 with every higher-ranked signal).
  \item Found that turnover decides net Sharpe: slower signals cut costs from 57--87\% to 1.5--3.5\% of gross PnL in local
        tests.
  \item Cycle 5: identified the hidden target as roughly a z-scored forward 120-hour return (correlation 0.93) and found two
        new edges, per-asset trend persistence and market lead/lag. Built a novelty model from 106 simple signals and
        their pairwise combinations that matched all 10 of the site's novelty verdicts.
  \item Cycle 5: traced an overfitting-gate failure to slow features that stay smooth under label shuffling; rebuilt the signal
        so it beat all 100 shuffled retrains in the hardest test months, with predictions unchanged (to 1e-14) under row
        padding and pickle reload.
\end{itemize}
